THE BROKEN BRIDGE

The bridge never ends. Keep walking – something is already on it with you.

1. Wear headphones.
2. Tap Begin, then slide the phone into your headset.
3. Walk in place (or take real steps) – every step moves you forward. Turn your head to look around.
4. Testing without a headset: drag to look, hold W or tap the screen to auto-walk.

Play in a clear, safe space with someone nearby. Contains flashing lights, loud sounds and jumpscares – not for people with photosensitive epilepsy or heart conditions.
